About

Curated Environment started with the fusion of the love between an architect and interior designer making their first property a home. Creating a legacy to follow in their footsteps was inevitable. Daughter, interior designer Ange de la Mar. Excited to co-create with clients to engage in a life changing role to produce a zen atmosphere to rest and retain inner peace at home or create a synergy of genius in a commercial space.

Our goal as a company is to inspire and create global economical awareness in cultivating design.
